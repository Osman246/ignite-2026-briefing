So I went through the Day 1 announcements and turned them into five shifts business leaders should plan for, each with one thing to do on Monday:

1️⃣ AI agents get a line manager. New tools track AI spend, success rates and hours saved. Name an owner for AI spend now.

2️⃣ Copilot becomes where the workday starts. Word, Excel and PowerPoint now work inside Copilot. Test three everyday workflows with one team.

The reality check:
📈 AI use among US firms rose from 17% to 24% in a year
🏢 It ranges from 45% in information firms to 10% in mining
👥 Only 2.5% of firms say AI reduced their headcount

The agent era is still in the planning stage for most businesses. That gives early movers an advantage.
